# Disclaimer

PreciMind QC Learning Lab is **educational and simulation software**.

- It is **not a medical device**.
- It is **not clinical decision-support software**.
- It is **not intended to be used for actual patient-care decisions**.
- It does **not replace** laboratory standard operating procedures,
  manufacturer instructions for use, regulatory requirements,
  professional judgment, or institutional policy.
- It does **not provide certification of professional competence**.
- It should **not be used for employment-performance assessment** —
  the software's own instructor-analytics workspace explicitly labels
  itself "Simulation-learning analytics only. Not a measure of
  clinical competence or employment performance," displays no ranking
  or leaderboard, and makes no pass/fail certification claim.

Use PreciMind to practice and reason about quality-control concepts in
a simulated environment. Always follow your institution's actual
policies, your instruments' actual manufacturer instructions, and
applicable regulatory requirements in real laboratory practice.
